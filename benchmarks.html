Evaluation methodology

SI: measure constituted population, recall and sampled correctness with exact denominators, evidence coverage, export parity, elapsed time and hardware. DA: measure exact action refusal/execution, false block/escalation, protected effect observations, latency distribution, throughput and trust configuration. Compare like workloads and define boundaries.
