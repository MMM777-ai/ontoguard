Exact-action authorization case study

Reviewed payment integration and separately recorded v1.5.2 local mock execution explain the boundary from approval to verified effect.

What has been independently reviewed?

AgenTrust PR #201, merged September 20, 2026: 27 adapter tests in maintainer reproduction; the exact $250,000 payment committed once with TRACE, while the changed $260,000 path was refused with zero commits and no TRACE. PR #236, merged October 6, 2026 after the October 3 review: 36 integration tests, 97 negative wrapper/direct-executor probes refused without protected effect and 4 positive executions. These validate bounded integrations, not the whole proprietary runtime or all routes.

How does authorization differ from execution?

In a separate v1.5.2 controlled local mock-target run, the changed amount returned HTTP 409 with no observed mock commit, a tampered consume acknowledgement was refused, and an exact verified consume produced one recorded local effect. The pre-effect packet reports CONSUMED_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN (checkpoint 4); the exact-effect packet reports EXECUTED_EFFECT_FORMED (checkpoint 5) after an accepted signed outcome receipt. The former is not proof of no effect. Both historical checks used development test trust; V16 current freshness was NOT RUN. No real financial transfer, independent production log or customer deployment is claimed.
