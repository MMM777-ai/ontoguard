Product capability · Decision Authorization

An approved action must still be the exact action that executes.

Exact action, current authority, protected execution

OntoGuard checks trusted authority and the material action binding at the protected execution boundary. A changed target, amount or required state cannot inherit a stale ALLOW.

View the complete productInspect the evidence

Why it matters

Protect high-impact routes against substitution and stale authority; evaluate positive and negative cases in the actual deployment corridor.

Discuss your evaluation

Before the consequential effect

Typed action schema, no implicit coercion, trusted provider separation and cryptographic binding connect authorization to the proposed action. Re-check immediately before protected effect where configured, especially if anything changes: expiry, revocation, state, authority or proof.

Exact action→Trusted context→Authorize→Re-check→Consume→Effect

Across platforms, each protected target or commit boundary must participate. An external worker, gateway, service or target-side adapter may provide the integration seam. Universal interception is not claimed.
