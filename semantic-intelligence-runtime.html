Why OntoGuard

The combination that is difficult to replicate

01 · Differentiator

Meaning you can defend

Every visible qualified concept must have a real semantic identity, a definition, a domain, a category, standing and exact supporting source evidence. This is more than extracting names or generating plausible descriptions.

Business value: Reduce time spent tracing definitions and explaining decisions to data owners and auditors.

Beyond glossary extraction: The runtime interprets supported actors, events, objects and relationships in context, using bounded domain knowledge where applicable. A raw field name is not automatically a concept; different meanings remain distinguishable.

02 · Differentiator

One ontology, disagreements included

Competing meanings, uncertain classifications and unresolved conflicts stay visible. Business and technical concepts coexist without collapsing different senses or treating absence of evidence as false.

Business value: Avoid silently propagating inconsistent definitions into reports, models and agents.

03 · Differentiator

Business obligations, not just field names

The finance-first, domain-extensible model captures who owes what, to whom, under what conditions, with relevant amount, currency, time, lifecycle and lineage when supported. A source assertion is not legal discharge or authority.

Business value: Make contract, lending, payments and other operational information more usable without manufacturing facts.

04 · Differentiator

Three levels of truth

What a source says, what it means, and what is actually in force or authorized are separate. Unknown settlement or fulfillment stays unknown until appropriate evidence exists.

Business value: Prevent a status label from becoming an unsupported business conclusion.

05 · Differentiator

Contracts that govern meaning

Generate native semantic data contracts for eligible fields from a sealed revision. Strict validation, receipts, staleness and drift checks distinguish a changed definition from an unchanged physical data type.

Business value: Catch meaning changes before they flow silently into downstream reporting or integrations.

06 · Differentiator

One version, every representation

A governed semantic asset drives UI, API and export projections. Qualified input profiles and all 17 output formats, with release-reported cross-format parity checks, make the meaning reusable.

Business value: Reuse the asset across existing catalog, graph, BI and application tools rather than rebuilding maps for each consumer.

The defensibility is in the integrated assurance across the entire semantic asset, not a claim that any individual parser, ontology tool, graph or validation feature is unique.
