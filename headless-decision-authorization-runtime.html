A missing control point in agentic workflows

The decision is not the action

01

Propose

An AI agent, employee or workflow proposes a specific change.

02

Approve

The human or trusted business policy owns the actual clearance or approval.

03

Authorize & recheck

OntoGuard checks the exact change and trusted current conditions, again before protected effect.

04

Execute & prove

The trusted route commits or refuses the effect and records the observed outcome.

Human-in-the-loop remains. In sanctions operations, for example, the bank reviewer makes the clearance decision. OntoGuard does not detect sanctions or replace that reviewer. It checks whether the resulting specific protected action still matches authority and state when the bank executes it.

Why OntoGuard

Why the full control chain is difficult to replicate

01 · Differentiator

Permission bound to the exact action

Bind the actor, operation, target and material parameters—not a broad approval, token or model score. Changing a material amount, recipient or target invalidates the earlier binding.

Business value: Reduce exposure to substituted or manipulated payments, record changes and privileged tool calls.

02 · Differentiator

Authority comes from trusted systems

Use controlled policy, authority, evidence and current resource state rather than claims supplied by an agent. Keep the actual business decision with its authorized human or system owner.

Business value: Keep existing approvals and accountability intact across agentic workflows.

03 · Differentiator

Check again immediately before effect

An earlier ALLOW is not permanent permission. Re-evaluate current conditions at the protected execution point; missing or changed required authority is refused or routed for review.

Business value: Close the gap between approval and execution when circumstances change.

04 · Differentiator

Fail closed at the protected boundary

A trusted executor verifies the exact signed, anchored consume acknowledgement before a protected effect. A decision endpoint alone is not enforcement; all relevant routes must be controlled for the desired protection.

Business value: Make the wrong action fail at the route that matters, not just generate an alert.

05 · Differentiator

Recovery without inventing an outcome

Idempotent retry, reconciliation and explicit unresolved outcomes distinguish a consumed authorization from an observed effect. Missing receipt evidence is not treated as no effect.

Business value: Support incident investigation and recovery without turning an ambiguous retry into a new business action.

06 · Differentiator

Proof another party can verify

Correlate authorization, pre-effect consume, reasons and a separate signed outcome. Portable packets and a standalone verifier support verification without trusting the live runtime; independent production log and pinned trust are deployment requirements.

Business value: Give audit, risk and customer teams a defensible approval-to-effect record within the covered route.

The moat is the combination of control, execution and proof. It is not the ALLOW/BLOCK/ESCALATE endpoint alone. The customer retains the execution route, credentials, authority sources and independent trust deployment.

Durability and privacy are part of the moat, too. In the qualified zero-window design, final acknowledgement and protected consumption depend on independently anchored, verified evidence—not merely a locally saved decision. Recovery keeps unresolved outcomes explicit; a rollback or fork detected against the declared authoritative trust model fails closed. Shareable packets use commitments rather than exposing raw sensitive action parameters. A standalone verifier checks evidence under pinned trust, with online checks required for current authoritative freshness. Production requires a separately operated log and a correctly integrated trusted executor. Explore evidence, recovery and trust boundaries.

What is exact-action authorization?

The runtime evaluates permission for a specific typed action on a specific target under trusted authority and current state. Human approval is not permanent permission to execute a changed action. Revalidation and trusted executor enforcement are required at the protected route; no blanket interception or customer deployment is implied.
