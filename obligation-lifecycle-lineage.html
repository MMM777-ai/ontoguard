Submission is not settlement

A policy may define a repayment obligation, a source may record submission and another may establish settlement. Track parties, conditions, timing and provenance independently; missing later evidence remains unknown. Amendments or reversals require separate history. The semantic asset does not issue a legal determination or authorize a payment. Lifecycle semantics.

What did the native lineage example establish?

The v1.89.332 controlled lineage dataset contains eight concepts, seven candidate graph edges and sixteen exact source locators. Synthetic records preserve SYN-PMT-01 and SYN-LOAN-01; the runtime returns candidate INTENDED_TO_FULFILL between Payment Submission and Repayment Obligation. Matching identifiers or dates alone do not establish canonical same-transaction identity. Settlement, discharge and operative authority are UNKNOWN or not established. A semantic interpretation is not permission to act.
