ALLOW, BLOCK and ESCALATE for agent actions

Separate permission, refusal and review from execution outcome.

How it works

ALLOW permits the validated action within configured boundaries; it does not establish effect. BLOCK refuses a protected action; ESCALATE withholds it for review. No-formation and unknown outcome are separate from ordinary decisions. Human approval must be checked against the exact action at execution.

Illustrative explanations describe the boundary, not a customer result. For specific evidence and integration scope see the linked product page.

Decision and effect are separate

ALLOW indicates permission in scope, not a committed result. BLOCK or ESCALATE must not execute through a protected route; an unavailable required authority can prevent formation entirely. A later review resolution requires a new check when conditions change. Capture actual outcome separately and leave unknown outcomes unknown.

How does DA handle fail closed?
