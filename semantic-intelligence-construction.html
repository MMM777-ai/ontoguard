Source to constituted concept

Native source observations→Evidence & interpretation→ConceptSense→Typed relationships

Deterministic, native-first construction distinguishes a raw string or field from a constituted ConceptSense. Exact source locators support definitions and domains. Optional bounded provider assistance may supply evidence but does not govern simply because a model produced an answer.

One ontology, not a preferred subset

Each constituted visible concept has identity, populated evidence-grounded Definition and Domain, Concept Category and semantic standing. Categories include business, technical, structural, scientific, legal, operational, property-like, event-like and measurement-like. Class and Role are orthogonal dimensions and may be unknown. Needs Review remains visible within the one constituted ontology. In the controlled rich run, 22 visible constituted concepts include two review-required rows; the native accepted/counting summary is 20. Review standing is not a separate primary ontology.

General Ontology organizes concepts; the phenomenological layer records evidence and observations; selective micro-ontologies add specialized meaning. These are complementary layers, not separate products.

Open-world UNKNOWN is not false. Conflicting evidence remains visible, with alternatives and review requirements rather than silent promotion.
