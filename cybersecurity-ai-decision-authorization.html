From alert to controlled remediation

A SOC product owner selects one target-side remediation or sensitive export. The platform supplies trusted asset identity, operation, current policy and reviewer approval. A valid action and a changed target exercise the protected seam; a refusal is measured as no observed effect only with target evidence. Existing IAM, policy engines and gateways may already bind transaction details; evaluate whether pre-commit revalidation, unique consume, recovery and correlated proof add value. SI can supply evidence-qualified asset and policy semantics but not authority.
