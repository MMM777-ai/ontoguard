From loan semantics to a protected payment

A lending architecture owner supplies an approved dictionary, policy and schema. Identify distinct loan and payment meanings, conditions, amount/currency and source locators; inspect unresolved settlement status instead of inferring discharge. Deliver a governed graph, contracts and selected exports to the bank’s catalog. Separately, the payment owner selects one corridor with authoritative reviewer approval, account and amount binding, a protected executor and observable result. Test the exact approved action, a changed amount and stale approval; compare evidence collection effort and latency. The bank retains sanctions clearance and final decision ownership.
