Company

Decision authority for actions that bind the enterprise.

OntoGuard is built by Mark Starobinsky. Contact: mark.starobinsky@ontoguard.ai.

Research: Mark’s current work formalizes Decision Authorization and semantic institutional admissibility for agentic AI. Read the September 2026 preprint or view his ResearchGate profile. Preprint — not peer-reviewed publication.

OntoGuard uses structured semantic context to understand what an agent is attempting to change and how that movement relates to evidence, authority, policy, and institutional state. Ontology is part of the mechanism. The product is Decision Authorization.

The public proof surface includes a controlled Decision Authorization demonstration, sanitized packets, and an AgenTrust TRACE Community Integration that a maintainer exercised before merge. None of that is a claim of universal production enforcement.
