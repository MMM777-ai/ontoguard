About OntoGuard

Infrastructure for trust in enterprise information and actions.

OntoGuard builds two independent enterprise software runtimes: one constructs and assures business meaning, the other protects exact consequential actions. Our focus is usable software, evidence and measurable outcomes—not another generic governance dashboard.

Who stands behind the software

OntoGuard was founded by Mark Starobinsky. The company builds independent software runtimes for enterprise meaning and exact-action permission. Support and licensing terms are agreed for each engagement.

Engineering principles

What we believe

Meaning should be defensible

An enterprise should be able to see why a concept exists, where the definition came from and what remains unknown.

Permission should be enforceable and inspectable

Approval should bind the exact action, and execution evidence should distinguish what was permitted from what happened.

The two product families are separately licensable. Both are supported by controlled demonstrations and release-specific evidence; neither requires a claim of universal correctness or production-wide enforcement.

Two independent software assets

OntoGuard focuses on evidence-backed enterprise meaning and permission-to-act before consequential effects. The product families are independently deployable and licensable. Proof and explicit boundaries are common design principles.

Engineering focus

OntoGuard separates semantic meaning from operative permission. The SI runtime builds evidence-qualified concepts with source provenance and explicit unknowns; the DA runtime binds trusted permission to an exact consequential action and can independently verify evidence in configured deployments. Each has its own product evaluation, integration and licensing path. Public evidence includes controlled demonstrations and bounded reviewed contributions, not customer adoption claims.
