ALLOW / BLOCK / ESCALATE is the product language. A score is evidence, not authorization.

ESCALATE routes the action to qualified human authority and records reviewer, evidence considered, disposition, release state, and resulting consequence.

No-bind proof records that a protected effect did not form.

Ontology supplies structured semantic context so the system can interpret the attempted change. It is not the commercial category.

FAQ

Is OntoGuard a monitoring or guardrail product?

No. Monitoring observes. Guardrails constrain. OntoGuard determines whether an exact proposed transition may proceed and proves what happened afterward.

Do I need to upload internal documents to start?

No. Start with representative actions. Do not send confidential customer data through the public form.

Does the site claim production L5 everywhere?

No. Controlled proof is demonstrated. Customer-route production completeness requires integration evidence.

Is the AgenTrust integration Verified?

Yes. The integration is currently listed at the AgenTrust Verified tier. AgenTrust documents Verified as an end-to-end maintainer validation of the published integration against released packages. That status does not mean hardware attestation, customer production enforcement, or certification of the OntoGuard product as a whole.

Does OntoGuard replace human authority?

No. ESCALATE exists specifically to route to qualified human authority.

Decision Authorization demo (1:05)

AI agents can now move money and change records. The question is whether this exact action is authorized to happen. OntoGuard sits at that decision boundary. It evaluates the exact proposed action against evidence, governing basis, intent, and authority. The result is explicit: ALLOW, BLOCK, or ESCALATE to qualified human authority. Change something material — and the prior decision no longer binds. Authorization binds to the action that was actually evaluated. OntoGuard records the decision: release state, human-review requirement, and whether the protected consequence formed. A self-contained headless layer inside the enterprise execution environment.
