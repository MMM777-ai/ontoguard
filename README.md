# OntoGuard

**Understand the enterprise. Authorize the action. Prove the result.**

Official website: [https://ontoguard.ai/](https://ontoguard.ai/)

OntoGuard develops two independently licensable, OEM-embeddable enterprise infrastructure runtimes for data, AI, workflow and cybersecurity platforms. Semantic Intelligence constructs and assures enterprise meaning. Decision Authorization evaluates permission to perform exact consequential actions and preserves evidence of authorization and execution. Either runtime can be integrated independently; semantic intelligence does not itself confer permission to act.

## Why OntoGuard is hard to replicate

Many products help catalog information, generate definitions, validate schemas, approve workflows or log actions. OntoGuard's differentiator is the combination of independently implemented capabilities, not an unsupported claim that every competing product lacks each feature.

- **Semantic Intelligence:** automated evidence-qualified construction, distinct meanings and explicit unknowns, obligation and lifecycle semantics, sealed native contracts and consistent interoperable projections, with no dependency on an LLM.
- **Decision Authorization:** exact-action binding, trusted current business basis, pre-effect revalidation, fail-closed protected execution, recovery and correlated independently verifiable proof.

Enterprise and OEM buyers can evaluate either capability independently on their own business problem. Potential value includes lower manual reconciliation and audit effort, reusable semantic assets, reduced exposure to changed actions on protected routes, and reduced build/maintenance burden for platform teams. These are evaluation objectives, not guaranteed results.

## 1. OntoGuard Semantic Intelligence Runtime OEM

**Enterprise meaning. Built automatically. Backed by evidence. No LLM. Fully offline.**

The semantic core runs entirely inside your environment on CPU without an LLM, internet connection, GPU or cloud AI service. Air-gapped deployment is possible with locally provisioned dependencies. Optional qualified providers can augment evidence, but do not establish governing truth. No model token fees are required for core processing; total deployment costs still depend on the environment.

OntoGuard constructs one evidence-qualified enterprise ontology from fragmented data dictionaries, schemas, policies, glossaries and documents. It produces reusable semantic assets with source provenance, rather than treating every raw field or source assertion as an established business fact.

Key capabilities:

- Automated semantic construction, with definitions, domains, Concept Categories, classes where supported, semantic standing and exact source locators.
- Cross-source reconciliation, preservation of competing interpretations and explicit uncertainty, and multi-edge semantic relationships.
- Finance-first, domain-extensible obligation, lifecycle, lineage and consequence intelligence. Source assertions do not independently establish legal discharge, actual fulfillment or operative authority.
- Native Semantic Data Contracts bound to sealed semantic revisions; strict record validation, validation receipts, structural and semantic drift, staleness and bounded impact assessment.
- Private, CPU-based core processing without requiring an LLM, GPU, cloud AI or internet access.
- Qualified ingestion profiles and interoperable semantic exports, including graph and tabular formats, for existing enterprise platforms.

## 2. OntoGuard Headless Decision Authorization Runtime

**Legitimate access does not mean authorized action. The decision is not the action.**

Retain human and policy approval. Check the exact action again if anything changes. The accountable business owner makes the clearance or approval decision; OntoGuard controls the permission-to-act boundary through a properly integrated protected route. For an enterprise buyer, this means evaluating the actual payment, record change, case disposition or privileged tool operation—not just a model's output.

OntoGuard evaluates whether a particular actor may perform an exact consequential action on a specific target under trusted current authority, policy and state. It is designed for integration with existing AI agents, human approval workflows, security platforms and transaction execution systems.

Key capabilities:

- Exact-action binding and explicit ALLOW, BLOCK and ESCALATE outcomes, with separate handling of nonformation and standing.
- Revalidation of material action parameters, trusted authority and current conditions at a configured protected execution seam.
- Fail-closed handling of invalid, missing, stale or mismatched authorization on correctly integrated protected routes.
- Signed authorization, pre-effect consume and separate execution outcome evidence, with recovery, reconciliation and standalone proof verification.
- Integration with existing human-in-the-loop controls rather than assuming human approval automatically authorizes a materially changed action.

## 4. OEM licensing and enterprise evaluations

**Build versus license:** A platform that builds these capabilities itself must maintain the full semantic assurance chain or the full authorization-to-effect proof chain, not just a parser, model, approval step or decision endpoint. OntoGuard provides independently licensable runtimes; the OEM retains its product UX, authorized integrations and deployment responsibilities. Evaluate actual integration, maintenance and time-to-market economics before licensing.

OntoGuard is designed for independent OEM licensing and embedded integration into established enterprise products. Relevant applications include financial services, insurance, healthcare payers, cybersecurity, government and critical infrastructure, and enterprise data and AI platforms.

- **Semantic Intelligence:** evaluate representative sources, evidence-qualified concepts and relationships, semantic contracts and interoperability against the enterprise's own requirements.
- **Decision Authorization:** evaluate an exact protected action, trusted approval and state, changed-action refusal, execution outcome and verifiable evidence within the intended integration boundary.
