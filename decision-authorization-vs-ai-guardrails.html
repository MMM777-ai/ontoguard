Decision authorization vs AI guardrails

Guardrails may inspect content, context and tool parameters; capabilities vary by implementation.

How it works

Exact-action authorization integrates current trusted authority, pre-effect revalidation, execution seam and correlated outcome proof. These are complementary to content safety, IAM, policy engines and security monitoring, not replacements for every control.

Illustrative explanations describe the boundary, not a customer result. For specific evidence and integration scope see the linked product page.

Check capability overlap

Some guardrails inspect tool parameters and policy engines can authorize exact requests. Compare what is actually enforced: trusted state, action binding, immediate recheck, executor verification, recovery and proof. Content filtering alone is not evidence of authorized target commit; if all required controls already exist, an additional product may not be necessary.

What should a buyer compare?

ControlEvaluation question
Content and tool guardrailsCan the control inspect and restrict proposed tool arguments and malicious context?
IAM / OAuth / policy engineDoes it bind the particular transaction and trusted current authority, or only broad access?
Protected execution authorizationDoes the executor verify exact action and revalidation before effect, with correlated consume and outcome evidence?

Capabilities overlap. If existing controls satisfy these requirements, licensing another runtime may not be necessary. The OntoGuard evaluation focuses on missing protected-action controls and verifiable evidence. Read the reviewed adapter and local execution evidence.

Evidence and buyer questions

How does DA differ from guardrails?

Guardrails can inspect prompts, outputs and tool calls. Decision Authorization binds current permission to the exact consequential action and supports revalidation, protected consume and separate outcome evidence. It complements, rather than categorically replaces, guardrails.
