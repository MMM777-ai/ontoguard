What is an evidence-backed ontology?

An ontology organizes concepts and their relationships; an evidence-qualified ontology records why an interpretation is supported.

How it works

A definition may be supported by a dictionary row and contradicted by a policy. Preserve both locators and standing. Do not confuse a source assertion with reality or resolve conflict by choosing the most convenient wording. Distinct senses may share a label; stable IDs prevent merging.

Illustrative explanations describe the boundary, not a customer result. For specific evidence and integration scope see the linked product page.

Evidence is not infallibility

Two documents may both use Payment but one means submission and the other settlement. Keep identities distinct, show the exact assertions and avoid resolving a current fact from missing evidence. Definition, domain and category must be populated for constituted rows; optional class and role may be unknown. A review-required row stays visible. Inspect the illustrative fixture.

How is ontology evidence retained?

Exact source locators bind selected meanings to source assertions. Distinct senses can share a label; alternatives and review standing preserve conflict. The controlled rich sample has 45 exact workbook-cell locators, not proof of customer results.
