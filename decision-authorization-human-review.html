Product capability · Decision Authorization

Keep the human decision. Protect what happens after it.

Human approval to protected effect

Human reviewers retain business authority. OntoGuard checks that the exact consequential action matches that authority and remains valid when the protected system executes.

View the complete productInspect the evidence

Why it matters

Help reviewers and control owners inspect what was approved, what was attempted and what outcome was observed—without promising to remove human review.

Discuss your evaluation

A decision is not a later effect

Reviewer approves→Trusted approval evidence→Exact-action check→Revalidation→Commit or refusal

Illustrative bank case: a reviewer approves a specified $250,000 payment. A changed amount needs new authority; the prior approval is not inherited. The bank retains final clearance. OntoGuard does not detect sanctions or make legal decisions.

Structured reasons and evidence references can help reviewers examine what changed. No promise to eliminate human review, guarantee savings or deliver a prebuilt review dashboard.
