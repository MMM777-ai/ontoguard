Outcome-based evaluation

What you will know at the end

Semantic Intelligence

How much manual semantic engineering and reconciliation can the runtime replace or augment on your material? Can data owners inspect evidence, review conflicts, reuse the asset and validate key fields?

  • Baseline: hours to produce and reconcile definitions.
  • Measure: accepted concepts, provenance accuracy, export fidelity and review effort.
  • Deliverable: scoped semantic asset, evidence, gaps and supported contracts.

Decision Authorization

Can the actual protected route reject a materially changed action, while permitting the exact action that the enterprise authorized? Can audit inspect the separate outcome?

  • Baseline: review, evidence collection and integration effort.
  • Measure: accepted/refused cases, false escalations, latency and outcome coverage.
  • Deliverable: tested corridor, observed effects/refusals and explicit limitations.
ROI is buyer-owned: compare observed hours, loaded labor cost, integration and maintenance costs. Refused probes are not automatically financial losses avoided; no guaranteed ROI or autonomy level.

SI evaluation

Agree representative glossary/data dictionary, policy/document and schema, source rights, NDA/private processing, time and quality baselines, desired domains, review criteria, export and downstream use. Deliver an evidence-qualified asset, locators, graph, gaps/conflicts, sample contracts where applicable and measured comparison.

ROI inputs: manual semantic engineering hours × loaded cost, reconciliation/rework, export integration and validation. Deduct implementation and license costs. No guaranteed completeness or savings.

DA evaluation

Agree one protected action, budget/target owner, trusted approval/policy/state, protected route and credentials, positive execution and changed-condition refusal, outcome/no-effect evidence, latency and integration overhead. If no protected route is available, use controlled offline evaluation, not enforcement claims.

ROI inputs: measured refused unauthorized effects, false block/escalation, reviewer effort, audit collection and latency/integration costs. Refused probes are not automatically avoided losses.

Request DA evaluation

Measure value without inventing savings

SI: establish baseline manual semantic engineering and reconciliation hours, downstream integration and rework; compare measured hours and artifact acceptance after the scoped evaluation. DA: measure reviewer effort, refusal and false escalation, audit collection and integration/latency overhead. Estimate net benefit only from buyer inputs: avoided hours × loaded labor cost minus integration, license and maintenance costs. A refused attempt is not necessarily an avoided financial loss.

Evidence and buyer questions

What is a protected evaluation?

A focused evaluation defines buyer-owned inputs, scope and measurable success before integration. For SI assess source coverage and semantic/export fidelity; for DA evaluate one protected corridor, changed-action refusals, effect evidence and overhead. Results are not guaranteed savings.
