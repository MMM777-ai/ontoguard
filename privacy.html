Website privacy

The public explorers are read-only. No analytics, session replay or public runtime upload is enabled.

Website and inquiry data

Explorers load static same-origin evidence; they do not send documents to either runtime or execute actions. Do not enter confidential information, credentials or payment details.

The contact form prepares a draft in your browser. It does not send inquiry data to a website backend. When you choose to send the draft in your email application, your email provider and OntoGuard process the message.

The draft may contain your name, organization, business email, selected product, purpose and optional goal. No server-side inquiry processing is enabled in this website variant. The actual hosting logs, email provider and retention arrangements remain subject to the website owner’s prepublication review. Do not include confidential material.

For access, correction or deletion requests contact mark.starobinsky@ontoguard.ai. No analytics or session replay scripts are enabled in this website build.

Evidence and buyer questions

How is data handled in website demos?

Public demonstrations are read-only projections and recorded evidence, not uploads or a live private runtime. Do not submit confidential source data or protected action details. Website contact handling is described separately in the privacy notice.
