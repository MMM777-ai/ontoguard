Website privacy

The public explorers are read-only. No analytics, session replay or public runtime upload is enabled.

Website and inquiry data

Explorers load static same-origin evidence; they do not send documents to either runtime or execute actions. Do not enter confidential information, credentials or payment details.

The inquiry form sends your name, organization, business email, product, inquiry type, optional goal, consent and source page to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party service that delivers the message by email to mark.starobinsky@ontoguard.ai. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions for 30 days. OntoGuard retains messages in its mailbox for correspondence. Do not include confidential material.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes request data under its own privacy statement. Cloudflare Email Routing receives and forwards mail for ontoguard.ai; FormSubmit relays form inquiries. OntoGuard does not keep separate website access logs. Inquiries are retained in its mailbox for correspondence. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact OntoGuard. Do not include confidential material.

For access, correction or deletion requests contact use the contact form. No analytics or session replay scripts are enabled in this website build.

Evidence and buyer questions

How is data handled in website demos?

Public demonstrations are read-only projections and recorded evidence, not uploads or a live private runtime. Do not submit confidential source data or protected action details. Website contact handling is described separately in the privacy notice.
