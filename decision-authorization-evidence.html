Why it matters

Evidence chain

Signed authorization→Pre-effect consume→Trusted executor→Execution receipt→Reconciliation

Idempotency and recovery standing help distinguish unresolved outcomes from completed effects. System-of-record reconciliation detects discrepancies within observed coverage; it is not prevention of every alternate route.

In correctly configured production trust mode, required evidence must be independently anchored and verified before acknowledgement/protected execution; unavailable required proof cannot yield success. Reference-log qualification is not evidence of an external production log deployment. Historical integrity, current freshness, rollback and fork checks are distinct.

Proof disclosure uses approved scope and pinned trust identities. Browser checksum verification is not signature, independent-log or freshness verification.
